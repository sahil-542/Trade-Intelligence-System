========================================================================
PETROLEUM TRADE INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM - DAX MEASURES DOCUMENTATION
========================================================================

This document outlines all Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) used in the 
Power BI Dashboard. Each measure is categorized by its business use-case.

--- 1. CORE VOLUME METRICS ---
These measures calculate the foundational physical quantities of petroleum traded in Thousand Metric Tonnes (TMT).

Total Trade (Qty)
Use: Calculates the total physical volume of petroleum across both imports and exports.
DAX: Total Trade (Qty) = SUM('Fact_PetroleumTrade'[quantity])

Total Imports (Qty)
Use: Filters the total trade volume strictly to inbound flows (imports).
DAX: Total Imports (Qty) = CALCULATE([Total Trade (Qty)], 'Fact_PetroleumTrade'[import_export] = "IMPORT")

Total Exports (Qty)
Use: Filters the total trade volume strictly to outbound flows (exports).
DAX: Total Exports (Qty) = CALCULATE([Total Trade (Qty)], 'Fact_PetroleumTrade'[import_export] = "EXPORT")

Net Trade (Qty)
Use: Determines the trade balance (surplus or deficit) in physical terms.
DAX: Net Trade (Qty) = [Total Exports (Qty)] - [Total Imports (Qty)]


--- 2. TRADE PROPORTIONS & RATIOS ---
These measures analyze the distribution and balance between imports and exports.

Import Share %
Use: Computes the percentage of total trade that is comprised of imports.
DAX: Import Share % = DIVIDE([Total Imports (Qty)], [Total Trade (Qty)], 0)

Export Share %
Use: Computes the percentage of total trade that is comprised of exports.
DAX: Export Share % = DIVIDE([Total Exports (Qty)], [Total Trade (Qty)], 0)

Import to Export Ratio
Use: Shows how many times imports exceed exports (e.g., 1.36x). Critical for assessing energy dependency.
DAX: Import to Export Ratio = DIVIDE([Total Imports (Qty)], [Total Exports (Qty)], 0)


--- 3. FINANCIAL METRICS ---
These measures evaluate the monetary value of the petroleum trade in US Dollars (Millions) and Indian Rupees (Crores).

Total Trade Value (USD M)
Use: Calculates the total monetary value of trade in USD.
DAX: Total Trade Value (USD M) = SUM('Fact_PetroleumTrade'[dollars])

Total Import Value (USD M)
Use: The financial cost of importing petroleum (USD).
DAX: Total Import Value (USD M) = CALCULATE([Total Trade Value (USD M)], 'Fact_PetroleumTrade'[import_export] = "IMPORT")

Total Export Value (USD M)
Use: The financial revenue from exporting petroleum (USD).
DAX: Total Export Value (USD M) = CALCULATE([Total Trade Value (USD M)], 'Fact_PetroleumTrade'[import_export] = "EXPORT")

Total Trade Value (INR Cr)
Use: Calculates the total monetary value of trade in local currency (INR Crores).
DAX: Total Trade Value (INR Cr) = SUM('Fact_PetroleumTrade'[rupees])

Total Import Value (INR Cr)
Use: The financial cost of importing petroleum (INR).
DAX: Total Import Value (INR Cr) = CALCULATE([Total Trade Value (INR Cr)], 'Fact_PetroleumTrade'[import_export] = "IMPORT")

Total Export Value (INR Cr)
Use: The financial revenue from exporting petroleum (INR).
DAX: Total Export Value (INR Cr) = CALCULATE([Total Trade Value (INR Cr)], 'Fact_PetroleumTrade'[import_export] = "EXPORT")


--- 4. COMMODITY & PORT ANALYSIS METRICS ---
These measures filter out aggregated summary rows (like "PRODUCT IMPORT") to prevent double-counting when analyzing individual commodities.

Commodity Trade (Excl Summaries)
Use: Analyzes pure commodity-level volume by excluding macro summary rows from the PPAC data.
DAX: Commodity Trade (Excl Summaries) = CALCULATE([Total Trade (Qty)], 'Dim_Product'[is_summary] = "No")

Export Trade (Excl Summaries)
Use: Analyzes pure refined commodity exports, avoiding double counting.
DAX: Export Trade (Excl Summaries) = CALCULATE([Total Exports (Qty)], 'Dim_Product'[is_summary] = "No")

Port Trade Share %
Use: Computes the relative market share of a specific port against the entire selected maritime traffic.
DAX: Port Trade Share % = DIVIDE([Total Trade (Qty)], CALCULATE([Total Trade (Qty)], ALLSELECTED('Dim_Port')), 0)

Product Trade Share %
Use: Computes the relative market share of a specific petroleum product against all selected products.
DAX: Product Trade Share % = DIVIDE([Commodity Trade (Excl Summaries)], CALCULATE([Commodity Trade (Excl Summaries)], ALLSELECTED('Dim_Product')), 0)


--- 5. SYSTEM COUNTS & KPIs ---
These DAX measures power the high-level summary cards on the dashboard.

Number of Ports
Use: Distinct count of active ports facilitating trade during the selected period.
DAX: Number of Ports = DISTINCTCOUNT('Fact_PetroleumTrade'[port])

Number of Products
Use: Distinct count of active petroleum commodities traded (excluding aggregates).
DAX: Number of Products = CALCULATE(DISTINCTCOUNT('Fact_PetroleumTrade'[product]), 'Dim_Product'[is_summary] = "No")

Number of Financial Years
Use: Distinct count of financial years represented in the current filtered view.
DAX: Number of Financial Years = DISTINCTCOUNT('Fact_PetroleumTrade'[Year])

Active Months Count
Use: Distinct count of reporting months.
DAX: Active Months Count = DISTINCTCOUNT('Fact_PetroleumTrade'[Month])
